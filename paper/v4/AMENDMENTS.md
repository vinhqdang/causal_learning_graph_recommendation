Source: results/v6/prior_anatomy.txt, tests.txt, sensitivity.txt, reconcile.txt, bootstrap_rank.txt, tables.txt (all generated by experiments/v7_*.py); paper/v4/tables/T4..T11 are generated, do not retype numbers.

1. Thesis wording. The recommended title in DESIGN section 4 is NOT supported. Use: "What carries the gains of debiased graph recommendation? A controlled study on four benchmarks with unbiased test data" (alternatives 2 of DESIGN section 4 style). Claims:
   - Graph-specific debiasing adds little over an equally tuned backbone (cite A2 numbers).
   - The active ingredient of everything we built is an additive item-level prior fitted on the exposed log and added to the score. A shuffled item prior destroys it (Coat iALS-p -14e-3 vs none; KuaiRec iALS -112e-3), popularity does not reproduce it (pop is about equal to no prior for iALS; KuaiRec pop +1.1e-3 vs add +14.7e-3), but removing the IPS weighting does NOT hurt: add_noips is at least as high as the IPS prior in 6 of 8 cells and significantly higher on KuaiRec iALS (+8.1e-3, p_Holm < 0.001), KuaiRand prior-alone (+19.9e-3) and KuaiRec prior-alone (+15.2e-3). So the IPS part of the prior is not shown to matter; do not claim IPS or doubly robust estimation is needed.
   - On KuaiRec the training-free prior alone without IPS (0.6367) is within 0.0011 of DRUP (0.6378); report this as a finding about simple priors, with the TOST/CI from the tables (do not say equal unless the test says so).
   - The offset-only effect over LightGCN exists on Coat (+32.0e-3, pHolm 0.008) and KuaiRec (+21.2e-3) but not on Yahoo (-1.4e-3, pHolm 0.48) or KuaiRand (-0.8e-3, pHolm 0.47): there the gains of the Q weighting/refinements come from elsewhere or are not detectable.
   - Estimated propensities do not remove the Coat gain (X2: LightGCN-p 0.6025 est. vs 0.6020 shipped; iALS-p 0.6009 vs 0.5984, both differences ns). The LightGCN row with estimated propensities was not run; do not claim it.
   - Wider iALS grid on Coat: iALS-wide2 0.5922 vs stored iALS 0.5812 (+12.9e-3, pHolm 0.003), and the wide grid is not exhausted (alpha 1000 chosen in 4 of 10 splits). The earlier "iALS + prior" advantage over iALS (+16.8e-3, pHolm 0.27 vs the stored iALS) shrinks vs wide2 (-3.9e-3 for wide2 minus prior row, ns) -- report as: tuning budget alone closes most of the gap on Coat.
   - Selection-on-test inflation (A1): validation-selected vs test-oracle on Coat averages +0.022 (range +0.010 to +0.030, n=17 methods); Yahoo +0.004, KuaiRand +0.001, KuaiRec +0.001. The Coat inflation is as large as the method differences claimed in the literature there.
   - R0: re-selected bank means reproduce the stored means for 150 of 153 (file, method) pairs within 0.002; the 3 exceptions are legacy unsplit v3 files not used in the study (cause: patience not stored). State this in the appendix.
   - Coat P1 sign depends on the weighting unit (split-mean +4.1e-3 vs per-user -0.65e-3): report both and say which one the paper uses (per-user).
   - Bootstrap rank (A3): leader and P(rank 1) per dataset from results/v6/bootstrap_rank.txt; Coat has no stable leader (best P(rank 1) = 0.32).
