\section{Experimental details}\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Training-free operators.}
All operators share the score $s=s_1/c_1+\beta s_3/c_3$ (Section~\ref{sec:drup}) with
$\beta\in\{0,0.03,0.1,0.3,1,3,10\}$ or the three-hop term alone; the five-hop
variants add $\gamma s_5/c_5$ with $\gamma\in\{0.03,0.1,0.3,1,3\}$. The grids are:
normalisation exponent $\alpha\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$ (Coat), $\{0.5,0.7\}$
(Yahoo!\,R3, KuaiRand) and $\{0.3,0.5\}$ (KuaiRec); clip
$\tau\in\{0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1,0.2\}$ (Coat), $\{0.1,0.2\}$ (Yahoo!\,R3),
$\{0.02,0.05,0.1\}$ (KuaiRand) and $\{0.05,0.1,0.2\}$ (KuaiRec); imputation ridge
$\lambda\in\{1,5,20\}$ (Coat), $\{20\}$ (Yahoo!\,R3, KuaiRand) and $\{5,20\}$
(KuaiRec); additive or low-rank (rank 32) imputation; control-variate weight
$\in\{0,0.25,0.5,1\}$ on Yahoo!\,R3, $\{0,0.5,1\}$ on KuaiRand and $1$ elsewhere; and degree source $\in\{\hat Y,\ \text{cross-fitted }W\}$ for
the debiased operators. The degree source is a searched hyper-parameter.
$\hat Y$-degrees satisfy Assumption~\ref{as:fixed} whenever $\hat Y$ does;
cross-fitted $W$-degrees do not, since the weight at a pair then depends on
the exposures of other pairs, so configurations with $W$-degrees (selected on
Yahoo!\,R3) lie outside the theorems. Only $\hat Y$-degrees make the
certificates of Theorem~\ref{thm:robust} informative. EASE uses
$\lambda\in\{10,100,1000\}$; GF-CF uses four settings of (ideal low-pass weight, singular vectors):
$(0,0)$, $(0.3,64)$, $(0.3,256)$ and $(1,256)$. BSPM~\cite{choi2023bspm} uses blurring time
$\in\{1,2\}$, sharpening time $\in\{0,0.1,0.3\}$ and ideal low-pass weight
$\in\{0,0.3\}$ with one Euler step each. On the DR graph, EASE, GF-CF and BSPM
search the same nuisance grid as DR adjacency. The sample-split variants are
run for five draws of the split-off pairs; each draw is tuned separately, and
the tables report the mean over draws. The propensity model is
the one shipped with Coat and a logistic exposure model on user and item
exposure rates elsewhere; on Yahoo!\,R3 it was selected on validation data
against an outcome-dependent Naive-Bayes model. All nuisances are cross-fitted
over ten random folds of pairs, except for the sample-split variants, whose
imputation (and propensity model, where it is estimated) is fitted on a random
20\% of the pairs.

\paragraph{Trained models.}
The grid combines the learning rates $10^{-2}$, $3\cdot10^{-3}$ and $10^{-3}$ with the
weight decays $10^{-5}$, $10^{-4}$ and (Coat, and the MF models on Yahoo!\,R3)
$10^{-3}$. Embeddings have 64 dimensions (Coat: 32 or 64). The graph models with
the widest search (LightGCN and r-AdjNorm on Yahoo!\,R3, the pointwise
and BPR LightGCN on KuaiRec, all graph models on Coat) also search one to three
layers and 64 or 128 dimensions (Coat: 32 or 64); the others use two layers.
PDA searches $\gamma\in\{0.1,0.2\}$, r-AdjNorm $r\in\{0.3,0.7\}$, SimGCL the
contrastive weight $\in\{0.1,0.5\}$ (noise $0.1$, temperature $0.2$, InfoNCE over at
most 2{,}048 users and items of a batch) and MACR $c\in\{0.1,0.3\}$; iALS searches
the confidence $\alpha\in\{3,10,30\}$ and the ridge $\in\{1,10,100,1000\}$. DR-JL and MRDR
learn an MF imputation model jointly with the prediction
model~\cite{wang2019drjl,guo2021mrdr}. BPR models draw one uniformly sampled
negative per positive. Batch sizes are 256 (Coat), 1{,}024 (Yahoo!\,R3), 8{,}192
(KuaiRand) and 32{,}768 (KuaiRec, with 2M sampled pairs per epoch for the
pointwise losses); training runs for at most 100, 60, 40 and 30 epochs. Because
the training log is the same for every split, each configuration is trained
once and evaluated after every epoch on the validation part of every split.
Each split stops on its own validation score (patience 5 on Coat and
Yahoo!\,R3, 3 otherwise) and selects its own configuration and epoch; training
continues until every split has stopped, and a split never uses epochs after
its own stopping point, so no split's selection depends on another split's
validation users, who may be its test users. The split-0 selection is
re-trained with three (KuaiRec, KuaiRand: two) further seeds to measure seed
variance. We did not run DICE, StableDR and methods that train on the uniform
data (CausE, AutoDebias): in our protocol the uniform data serve only for
selection and testing, for every method. Table~\ref{tab:configs} lists the
number of configurations each method was selected from.

\paragraph{Evaluation and tests.}
Coat splits each user's candidates (30\% validation), Yahoo!\,R3, KuaiRand and
KuaiRec split the test users (30\% validation), over 10, 5, 5 and 5 random splits.
Users without a positive candidate are skipped. For a pair of methods, each
test user's metric difference is averaged over the splits in which the user
is in the test part, and we report the mean difference over users with a
95\% $t$-interval and the paired $t$-test. The Holm family is every comparison
run for a dataset, with all references together (DRUP, DR adjacency, five-hop
DRUP and DRUP-split against every other method); the Wilcoxon signed-rank
$p$-values are adjusted over the same family. Within-reference adjustments are
in the result files. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test gives the same conclusions except for the
five-hop DRUP against DR comparison on KuaiRec ($p=0.66$ against 0.011) and for
DRUP-split against pointwise LightGCN on KuaiRec ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.48$ against $3\cdot10^{-4}$). The
corrected resampled $t$-test over split means~\cite{nadeau2003} is far more
conservative with five or ten splits and is reported in the result files.
All tests condition on the training log and the fitted nuisances.

\paragraph{Hardware and code.}
All experiments ran on four CPU cores with 16\,GB of memory, without a GPU.
Code, configurations and result files are available at the repository given
on the title page.
