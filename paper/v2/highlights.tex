\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Weights sampled negatives by the posterior that an unlogged pair is positive.
\item A fixed imputation-logit offset adds a gain without uniform training data.
\item Beats every graph baseline in mean nDCG on Coat, Yahoo R3, KuaiRand, KuaiRec.
\item Best overall on Yahoo R3 and KuaiRand; not above iALS or DR-JL on Coat or KuaiRec.
\item Shuffled-weight controls and seed repeats show which gains are robust.
\end{itemize}
