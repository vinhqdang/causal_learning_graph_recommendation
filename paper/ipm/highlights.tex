\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Inverse-propensity graph propagation is biased beyond one hop.
\item The repeated-walk bias grows as the inverse of the propensity clip.
\item DRUP removes this bias exactly for any number of hops, with no training.
\item One structure yields fairness, explanation, robustness and privacy results.
\item DRUP beats tuned GNN baselines on Yahoo! R3 and KuaiRec unbiased tests.
\end{itemize}
