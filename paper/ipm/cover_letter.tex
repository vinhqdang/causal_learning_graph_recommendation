\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Walk-unbiased doubly robust graph
propagation: provably fair, transparent, accountable and private
recommendation from exposure-biased logs'', for publication in
\emph{Information Processing \& Management}.

Graph-based recommenders propagate preferences over interaction graphs that
the platform's own exposure policy has already filtered. The manuscript shows
that the inverse-propensity and doubly robust corrections now used inside graph
propagation are themselves biased once propagation spans more than one hop,
and it gives the exact bias and a lower bound. It then proposes DRUP, an
operator that is exactly unbiased and edge-wise doubly robust for any number of
hops at no extra cost and without training. The same structure yields provable
properties: causal exposure invariance, exact and optimal explanations,
certified robustness to injected profiles, joint differential privacy and
exposure-capped allocation with a certified gap. Every claim is checked on Coat,
Yahoo!\ R3 and KuaiRec, whose test data are random or fully observed, against
tuned trained baselines. The manuscript also reports where the guarantees stop
holding.

We believe the work fits the journal's interest in trustworthy information
access and recommender systems. The manuscript is original, has not been
published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. Code and
results are publicly available. In line with the double-anonymised review
policy, the manuscript file contains no identifying information; author details
and declarations are on the separate title page.

\closing{Sincerely,}
